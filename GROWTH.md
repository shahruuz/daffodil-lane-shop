# Getting the new site in front of buyers

The site now has what these channels need (product feed, structured data, gift pages). Each step below is a one-time setup, in order of payoff.

## This week (holiday window is open now)

1. **Google Search Console.** Add `thedaffodillane.com` as a Domain property (verify with the DNS record Cloudflare offers to add for you), then submit `https://thedaffodillane.com/sitemap.xml`. Google starts indexing the gift guides and product pages.
2. **Google Merchant Center (free product listings).** Create an account with thedaffodillane@gmail.com, verify and claim the website, set shipping to US $5 flat / free over $35 and returns to 14 days, then add a product source → "Add products from a file" → "Enter a link to your file": `https://thedaffodillane.com/feeds/google-merchant.xml`, daily fetch. Your 10 products can then show in Google Shopping, Images and Search at no cost.
3. **Confirm the holiday cutoff.** The banner and FAQ say "order by December 12 for delivery before Christmas". Change it in `catalog.json` if your making and shipping time differs.
4. **Pinterest business account.** Claim the website, then Catalogs → add the same feed URL. Gifts for nurses, new-mom gifts and bookmarks are strong Pinterest searches in November and December.

## Next

5. **Instagram / Facebook.** Link posts straight to the gift guides (`/gifts/nurses/`, `/gifts/new-moms/`, `/gifts/book-lovers/`), not the home page; they convert better because the visitor lands on the right three or four items.
6. **Team orders.** Send the `/bulk-orders/` link to El Paso hospital unit managers, baby-shower planners and local book clubs. Nurses Week (May 6–12) is the big date for the nurse items; start outreach in March.
7. **Newsletter.** Sign-ups show in the dashboard (Sign-ups → Download CSV). When there are 50+, move them to a free email tool (MailerLite, Buttondown) and send one note per month: new designs, holiday cutoffs.
8. **Stripe promo codes.** Checkout already accepts promotion codes. Create one in Stripe (Product catalog → Coupons) for newsletter subscribers or a team-order discount.
9. **Reviews.** After the first orders, ask buyers for a photo and a line you can quote. Real reviews can then go on product pages (and into Merchant Center later).
